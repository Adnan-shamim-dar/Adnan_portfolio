Creator · systems engineering · 2026

Tandem

Private local AI across your own devices. It serves open models through an authenticated OpenAI-compatible API, and can split one model by layers across PCs, Android phones and iPhones to run models too large for any one device. Pairing and traffic are end-to-end encrypted (X25519, HKDF, AES-256-GCM). It's about 18,000 lines of code, with a 47-check end-to-end test suite.
